Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zoe Life Services LLC ("Zoe Life Services," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website. It applies only to this website and not to information collected offline or through other services.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, for example when you use our contact form or email or call us. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your preferred contact method
  • The type of care you are interested in and the message you write

Please do not send medical records or detailed health information through this website. If care details are needed, we will discuss them with you directly.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the site running. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests for care
  • To discuss and arrange services you ask about
  • To respond to people interested in working with us
  • To keep this website secure and working properly

We do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed to operate this website and respond to you, for example with our website hosting and email delivery providers, who process it on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of others.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and for our normal business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. However, no website or email transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information online.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us through this website by contacting us using the details below.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Links to other websites

This website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy
