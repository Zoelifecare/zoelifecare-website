About Zoe Life Services

Care That Feels Like Family.

We believe great care begins with listening. Zoe Life Services LLC provides compassionate, dependable non-medical home care for seniors and adults who need a helping hand with everyday life.

Our purpose is simple: to help the people you love stay safe, comfortable, and independent at home, with practical assistance, meaningful companionship, and respect for each person's choices. Whether your family needs help for a few hours or more extensive support, we work with you to find a care arrangement that fits.

Our Services

Non-medical support for everyday life

Every family's needs are different. Here is how our caregivers can help. Availability and care arrangements are confirmed during your consultation.

Personal Care

Assistance with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and everyday personal routines while respecting each individual's privacy and dignity.

Companionship

Friendly conversation, meaningful activities, social engagement, and emotional support to help reduce loneliness.

Respite Care

Temporary assistance that gives family caregivers time to rest, recharge, and attend to their own responsibilities.

Memory & Dementia Support

Patient, structured assistance with familiar routines and daily activities in a supportive environment.

Meal Preparation

Help preparing meals according to individual preferences and household routines.

Light Housekeeping

Assistance with everyday household tasks such as laundry, tidying, and maintaining a comfortable living environment.

Transportation & Errands

Assistance with transportation to appointments, shopping, and other everyday errands, subject to applicable authorization and service arrangements.

Post-Hospital Support

Non-medical assistance with everyday activities following a hospital stay or surgery.

Extended & Overnight Care

Longer visits, overnight assistance, and extended care arrangements based on individual needs and availability.

Non-medical home care only. Zoe Life Services provides non-medical assistance with daily living. We do not provide nursing, medical treatment, diagnosis, or licensed clinical services.
