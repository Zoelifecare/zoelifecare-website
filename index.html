Our Services

Support for everyday life

Explore the services ZOE plans to offer. Availability and care levels will be confirmed during your consultation.

Personal Care

Help with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and daily routines while preserving dignity.

Companionship

Conversation, engaging activities, social connection, and emotional support.

Respite Care

Short-term relief for family caregivers who need time to rest or attend to other responsibilities.

Memory & Dementia Support

Patient, structured assistance and familiar routines for individuals experiencing memory changes.

Meal Preparation

Assistance planning and preparing meals and supporting comfortable mealtime routines.

Light Housekeeping

Help with laundry, tidying, and maintaining a comfortable home environment.

Transportation & Errands

Assistance with appointments, shopping, and community outings, where available.

Post-Hospital Support

Non-medical assistance with daily activities following a hospital stay or surgery.

Extended & Overnight Care

Flexible scheduling for families needing longer visits, overnight support, or around-the-clock arrangements.
